How recognition works

Recognition celebrates meaningful reviewing milestones. Your progress is based on completed community reviews that meet the recognition rules; repeated reviews of the same physical stop don't multiply your distinct-stop count.

Explorer

Explore distinct physical stops through completed reviews. Explorer milestones are 5, 20, 50, and 100 stops. Once a milestone is permanently earned, it remains part of your private recognition history.

Keep reviewing eligible stops to reach the next Explorer milestone.

  • Explorer is earned through completed qualifying reviews at distinct physical stops.
  • Explorer milestones are 5, 20, 50, and 100 distinct stops.
  • Reviewing the same physical stop again does not create another distinct-stop credit.
  • Qualifying historical reviews can count, including reviews of stops that are no longer active.
  • Earned awards remain part of your private recognition history.
  • Current progress and permanent achievements are separate: progress can change, while an earned award is permanent.
