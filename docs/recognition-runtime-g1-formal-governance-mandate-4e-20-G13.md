## 6. Revocation and supersession

The same project-owner governance authority may revoke this mandate or supersede
it by an explicit attributable project decision referencing this stable identifier,
the affected scope, effective ordering/time and intended disposition. Preserve the
original record and complete later decision; do not silently edit old approval.
An assistant, runtime issuer, producer, journal or witness cannot revoke, extend
or supersede it by asserting ownership of an operational identifier.

Evidence must identify the owner instruction/approval and exact replacement or
revocation record. Absence of a revocation file alone does not prove current
validity when status is uncertain. Supersession is a new explicit governance
decision, not automatic acceptance of a newer document or policy version.

Revocation/expiration of an appointment denies future eligibility production under
it. No automatic retrospective change to already-issued authority rights or
committed effects is established here. Mandate revocation stops new governance
adoption/appointment actions under this mandate; consequences for a prior adopted
policy/appointment must be stated explicitly in the revoking decision. Unknown
applicability blocks dependent use, not presumed continued permission.

Existing policy/source/authentication/activation/scope revocation and expiry checks
remain independent and mandatory. In particular, G2's current policy-revocation
checks are not weakened. No revocation releases duplicate fences, restores a spent
grant or authorizes compensating mutation/re-execution.
