# 4e-20-G11: external G1 governance mandate establishment package

## 1. G10 blocker and package status

[G10](recognition-runtime-g1-independent-governance-mandate-verification-4e-20-G10.md)
found no actual independent governance mandate in available inspected evidence.
[G9](recognition-runtime-g1-independent-governance-mandate-4e-20-G9.md) specifies
requirements but does not establish one. G1 remains unadopted, no producer is
appointed, no permissions change and implementation remains stopped.

This package is a submission checklist and future verification procedure. It
contains no completed mandate, example approval, named appointee, signature,
appointment or invented authorization record. Its item labels organize evidence
requests only; they are not new durable formats, schemas or authority identifiers.

## 2. Required external mandate artifact and submission contents

An actual authoritative project-governance decision must be established outside
the runtime authority model. Supply its complete final artifact and the following
supporting evidence through an independently attributable project-governance
channel. Repository placement, a matching digest or a supplied signer's name
alone is not proof of authenticity or legitimate authority.

| Package item | Must be supplied | Acceptance condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| M1: actual mandate | Final decision identity/revision, complete text, project/installation scope and explicit disposition | A real adopted mandate, not G9/G11, a proposal, blank form or runtime declaration |
| M2: approving authority | Identified actor/body and evidence of its legitimate independent project authority to establish this mandate | Root of legitimacy resolves outside source/producer/runtime permissions; no self-certification |
| M3: policy authority | Exact G1 P/version/digest and action; explicit right to decide prospective adoption | Full policy text resolves and matches reviewed scope; no inferred latest version or actual G1 adoption implied by the mandate alone |
| M4: appointment authority | Identified permitted appointing authority, powers and limits under that policy | Explicit authority to appoint only the constrained producer, not arbitrary issuers or target writers |
| M5: timing | Actual approval/record time, effective start and finite end, with authoritative ordering evidence | No backdating; currently applicable authorization can be determined independently |
| M6: approvals and authentication | Actual approvals required by the governing rules, approver identities, authority, authentication/provenance and exact approved revision | Complete verifiable decisions; no assumed quorum, copied actor ID or generic architecture approval |
| M7: independence and conflicts | Role relationship evidence and explicit allowed/prohibited overlap plus conflict/recusal rules | Independence from each operational role in section 3 is demonstrated, not asserted by that runtime |
| M8: revocation/supersession | Authorized revoker, status source, effective ordering, complete applicable changes and effects on subordinate policy/appointments | Current mandate applicability is resolvable; missing status access cannot be treated as proof of no revocation |
| M9: scope restrictions | Explicit exclusions and preservation of existing authorities/security properties | All section 4 restrictions hold without broad catch-all powers |

All items are required for reconsidering the mandate gate. Governance chooses the
actual legitimate actor, approval method, intervals and conflict rules; this package
does not designate them or create a new signer/key/quorum. Supply verifiable
approval evidence, not private keys, credentials, TOTP material or bearer tokens.

## 3. Independence proof

Provide attributable evidence identifying the source issuer, prospective producer,
target mutation owner and executing runtime roles, and their relationship to the
governance authorizer. Explain the separate permission chain that entitles the
authorizer to establish the mandate without deriving it from those roles.

For each role, the future verifier must establish: who/what occupies it; whether
there is overlap with governance approvers; which independent authorization allows
any overlap; the applicable conflict/approval rules; and evidence those rules were
followed for this exact decision. Separate process names, contexts, signatures or
actor IDs are insufficient without legitimate permission and attribution.

No mandatory two-person rule is invented, and same-actor legality is not presumed.
If overlap is unexplained or its independent approval cannot be verified, the
submission does not pass. Approval solely through the source/producer/target/runtime
authority being authorized is self-authorization and must be rejected.

## 4. Exact G1 policy scope

The mandate may authorize governance decisions for **prospective G1 adoption and
constrained producer appointment only**, under the exact reviewed policy/action.
It cannot itself adopt G1, appoint the producer or permit execution merely by
being submitted. Those remain distinct separately authorized decisions.

Explicitly exclude retroactive eligibility/broadening, general writer capability,
arbitrary target mutation, transfer, re-delegation, renewal of execution authority,
retry and recovery-based authorization. Preserve original-holder-only action,
exact source/decision/operation/target/runtime/reservation/family-effect/witness
binding, one atomic family-effect/witness target transaction and permanent single-use
fences. All mandatory fresh authentication, approval/ACK, activation, stop,
dependency, physical/schema, history/claim/control/migration/readiness, reservation
and deadline checks remain. The 120-second authority ceiling stays unchanged.

Policy version changes cannot silently expand old authorities. Mandate expiry,
revocation and supersession must have explicit effects on existing policies and
appointments; no grace, cascading invalidation or continued applicability may be
assumed where the governing evidence leaves it unclear.

## 5. Producer appointment evidence: later separate decision

For a future appointment, supply a separate complete attributable decision with:

- Exact producer identity/role and protected original source-issuance context;
  no caller-selected factory, generic SQL provider or transferable appointment token.
- Mandate and confirmed policy-adoption references, exact P/version/digest, appointing
  actor and independent evidence of its appointment permission/approval.
- Exact installation/target/family/action scope and runtime/incarnation/profile/
  generation/owner/authentication/context constraints; no authority to manufacture
  arbitrary execution decisions or adopt historical ones.
- Actual decision/effective start/end, ordering and current revocation/supersession
  status, including explicit consequences for already-issued eligible sources.
- Prohibitions on self-appointment, appointing another producer without independent
  authorization, scope expansion and unauthorized re-delegation.

An appointment is **not required to exist to verify a mandate that merely empowers
future appointment**. At that gate, verify the appointment *power* and constraints.
Before treating any producer as appointed, require the actual separate appointment
and applicable prior G1 adoption evidence. Their absence means unappointed, not an
incomplete mandate if the mandate gate's own evidence is otherwise sufficient.
No appointment is supplied, created or presumed in G11.

## 6. Audit and durability requirements

The external evidence must durably attribute authorizer, mandate, approvals,
exact revision/policy, effective time, permitted appointment scope and all applicable
revocation/supersession facts. Preserve full referenced artifacts and their ordering,
not just hashes or links to unavailable records. Identify the authoritative source
and custodian through which a future verifier can establish authenticity/current
status without relying on the producer's account alone.

Preserve subsequent separate adoption/appointment decisions and their links to
the mandate. Future eligible sources must resolve those facts through original
G2 consent/issuance provenance; generic audit text cannot replace that binding.
This package defines no storage implementation, ingestion API, signature scheme,
audit codec or permission to write records. It does not promise access to external
services; any later verification must stay within its separately authorized access.

## 7. Future verification gate (defined, not executed)

The future gate must receive actual external evidence and perform these checks:

1. Inventory the exact supplied final artifacts/revisions and references. Distinguish
   requirements, drafts and designs from actual attributable governance decisions.
2. Verify M2/M6 authenticity and project legitimacy independently of issuer/producer/
   target/runtime claims. Unverifiable authorship or legitimacy cannot be repaired
   by having the producer append an audit record.
3. Verify mandate identity, authority to adopt G1 and appoint its constrained
   producer, policy/version/action limits and exact match to G9 requirements.
4. Verify independence, approval completeness and all permitted overlap/conflict
   conditions against actual evidence, not presumed owner privileges.
5. Verify current effective interval/order and complete authoritative revocation/
   supersession state. Unknown current applicability fails closed.
6. Verify no retroactive authority broadening, target-write grant, general authority,
   retry/recovery permission or change to existing semantics/deadlines is hidden
   in the mandate or its referenced terms.
7. Verify that future appointment authority is explicit and constrained. If an
   actual appointment is also presented, assess it separately only when that
   verification is authorized; mandate validity alone never marks a producer appointed.
8. Record an attributable verification result with exact evidence references and
   limitations: verified mandate, absent mandate, or insufficient/ambiguous evidence.
   Missing/unknown/conflicting conditions deny authorization; do not create facts
   to obtain a pass.

A positive mandate verification would permit consideration of a **separately
authorized prospective G1 policy-adoption operation only**. It would not implement
G1, approve an executor, appoint a producer, issue source eligibility or allow DML.
Subsequent technical/security implementation gates remain separately required.

## 8. No self-authorization

No runtime authority, original source issuer, prospective producer, target mutation
owner, execution decision, journal state or target witness can demonstrate that
it possesses the missing governance mandate merely through its own existence,
scope, ownership, consent or generated records. Source audit can reference a
verified mandate; it cannot originate the external governance root it relies on.
Neither this package nor G9/G10 nor an assistant's completion report is a mandate.

## 9. Next boundary and present status

> The next technical step is permitted only after an actual independent mandate
> exists and is externally supplied/recorded.

That condition is necessary, not sufficient for implementation or mutation. The
first boundary after supply is the separately authorized evidence-verification
gate in section 7, not runtime execution. G11 does not execute that future gate
or assert new external evidence exists. The implementation chain remains stopped.
G1 is unadopted; no producer is appointed and no permissions change.

## 10. Decision A: establishment package complete

**A - Establishment package complete; external mandate is the sole remaining
prerequisite for reconsidering the independent-mandate verification gate.** This
is not a claim that mandate supply alone completes the wider G1 adoption,
appointment or enforcer implementation/security chain.

G11 adds a concrete submission inventory (M1-M9), explicit acceptance conditions,
independence proof checklist, separate later appointment evidence, current-status
and durability requirements, and an ordered fail-closed verification procedure.
It therefore adds an actionable external evidence package rather than declaring
G9's similar specification to be authorization. No authority is designated, no
mandate is created/simulated and no external approval is claimed.
